THE WORKSPACE

Study, research, and planning.

01

Study by chapter

Organize Markdown and LaTeX notes by subject. Use spaced repetition to review a single chapter or a range, and export selected notes as a PDF.

02

Follow the literature

Discover arXiv papers, save articles for later, and shape suggestions with keywords and your reading preferences.

03

Plan your work

Keep research questions, tasks, and reading in one place. Use the daily planner to decide what fits the time you have.

FREE DESKTOP PREVIEW

Download PRISM.

Mac requires Apple silicon and macOS 13.5 or later. Move PRISM.app to Applications. On Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit), extract the ZIP and open PRISM.exe.

The preview uses an ad-hoc signature. If macOS asks for first-launch approval, follow the install guide for Open Anyway in System Settings → Privacy & Security.
